Sooth

A fictional mental health journaling app designed to make journaling effortless, private and emotionally supportive, especially when users have little time, energy or mental clarity.

Type
Concept project (fictional app)
Phase covered
UX research and design thinking

Problem

Journaling can support mental well-being, but many people struggle to keep the habit. Blank-page anxiety, perfectionism, lack of time and pressure to write long or meaningful entries get in the way.

Audience

  • Aiden, a graduate student who struggles with late-night anxiety and overthinking.
  • Priya, an emergency-room nurse and single parent who needs journaling to fit into very small pockets of time.

Design philosophy

The app should demand less from users precisely when they have the least to give.

  • Instant entry access
  • Adaptive prompts instead of an intimidating blank page
  • Support for very short, unfiltered entries
  • Privacy reassurance
  • Guilt-free journaling habits
  • Gentle, non-clinical reflections that help users notice patterns

What this phase covers

Research synthesis, personas, an emotional journey map, pain points, opportunities and How Might We statements that guide the product design phase.
